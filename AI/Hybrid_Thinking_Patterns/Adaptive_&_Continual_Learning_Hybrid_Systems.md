## Section 11: Adaptive & Continual Learning Hybrid Systems

| Hybrid Thinking Pattern | Combined Patterns | Where It Is Used | Description |
|-------------------------|-------------------|------------------|-------------|
| **Dynamic Curriculum Reflection (DCR)** | Curriculum Learning + Reflexion | Autonomous skill acquisition, coding agents | The agent reflects on its own success/failure rates and dynamically adjusts the difficulty curve of its training/interaction to optimize learning speed. |
| **Episodic Replay with Self-Correction (ERSC)** | Experience Replay + Intra-episode Self-Correction | Robotics, long-term personal AI assistants | Stores detailed memories of past errors, replays them during idle compute time, and generates verified corrections to use as heuristics in the future. |
| **Meta-Learning Prompt Optimization (MLPO)** | Continual Learning + Few-Shot Adaptation | Enterprise AI platforms, evolving knowledge bases | Continuously evaluates which few-shot reasoning examples yielded the best output, and updates its own prompt templates dynamically over its lifecycle. |
| **Exploration-Guided Chain of Thought (EG-CoT)** | Exploration-Exploitation Trade-off + Chain of Thought | Discovery models, scientific hypothesis generation | Balances generating standard logical deductions vs purposefully exploring low-probability reasoning branches to discover previously unseen combinations. |
| **Elastic Knowledge Consolidation (EKC)** | Continual Learning + Knowledge Graph Updates | Medical diagnosis systems, adaptive legal AI | Integrates new reasoning patterns while cross-referencing against a structured knowledge graph to explicitly prevent catastrophic forgetting of base rules. |
