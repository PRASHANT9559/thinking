## Section 10: Creative & Divergent Thinking

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Lateral Thinking** | Creative reasoning | Brainstorming, ideation, innovation | Solving problems through an indirect and creative approach, using reasoning that is not immediately obvious |
| **SCAMPER Technique** | Creative reasoning | Product design, creative prompt engineering | Systematically applying prompts (Substitute, Combine, Adapt, Modify, Put to another use, Eliminate, Reverse) to generate new ideas |
| **Six Thinking Hats** | Creative reasoning | Multi-perspective analysis, simulated debate | Directs the model to evaluate a problem from six distinct perspectives (facts, emotions, negative, positive, creative, control) iteratively |
| **Conceptual Blending** | Creative reasoning | Creative writing, metaphor generation, art | Taking attributes from two entirely different concepts and merging them to create a novel mental space or idea |
| **Random Stimulation / Forced Analogy** | Creative reasoning | Overcoming mental blocks, divergent brainstorming | Forcing a connection between the current problem and a completely randomly chosen word or object to spark new angles |
| **Opposite / Reverse Thinking** | Creative reasoning | Problem solving, strategic planning | Instead of thinking about how to achieve a goal, reasoning about how to guarantee failure, and then reversing those steps |
| **First Principles Reductive Brainstorming** | Creative reasoning | Disruptive innovation, engineering design | Breaking down a complex concept to its most fundamental truths, ignoring current conventions, and building back up creatively |
