## Section 7: Specialized Domain Reasoning

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Legal Reasoning** | Research concept | Legal AI, contract analysis | Applies statutory interpretation, precedent analysis, and legal logic to case analysis |
| **Medical Diagnostic Reasoning** | Research concept | Clinical decision support | Combines symptoms, history, and medical knowledge through differential diagnosis processes |
| **Scientific Reasoning** | Research concept | Research assistants, hypothesis generation | Forms hypotheses, designs experiments, analyzes data following scientific method principles |
| **Ethical Reasoning** | Research concept | AI ethics systems, value alignment | Applies ethical frameworks (utilitarian, deontological, virtue ethics) to moral dilemmas |
| **Financial Risk Reasoning** | Research concept | Trading systems, risk management | Probabilistic assessment of market conditions, portfolio risks, and scenario analysis |
| **Narrative/Story Reasoning** | Research concept | Creative AI, plot generation | Understands story structure, character motivation, and narrative causality |
| **Mathematical Reasoning** | Research concept | Formal math systems, proof assistants | Combines intuition with formal verification in mathematical problem solving |
| **Visual Reasoning** | Research concept | Multimodal AI, diagram understanding | Reasoning about visual information, charts, diagrams, and spatial layouts |
| **Code Reasoning** | Research concept | Program synthesis, bug detection | Understanding program semantics, execution flows, and code transformation |
| **Common-sense Physics Reasoning** | Research concept | Embodied AI, simulation | Reasoning about object permanence, gravity, fluids, and physical interactions |
