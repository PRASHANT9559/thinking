## Section 11: Adaptive & Continual Learning Patterns

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Curriculum Learning** | Adaptive reasoning | Training agents, autonomous skill acquisition | Structuring the reasoning tasks from easiest to most difficult to gradually build up competence without catastrophic forgetting |
| **Experience Replay** | Adaptive reasoning | Reinforcement learning bots, long-term memory agents | Storing past problem-solving traces and periodically reviewing them to reinforce successful patterns and avoid old mistakes |
| **Few-Shot Adaptation** | Adaptive reasoning | In-context learning, prompt engineering | Rapidly adjusting reasoning behavior based on just a small handful of newly provided examples within the current prompt scope |
| **Self-Correction (Intra-episode)** | Adaptive reasoning | Coding assistants, real-time agentic execution | Recognizing an error during the execution of a task and dynamically adjusting the plan before completing the final output |
| **Lifelong / Continual Learning** | Adaptive reasoning | Personal AI assistants, evolving knowledge bases | Accumulating knowledge and reasoning strategies continuously over a long lifespan without overwriting past fundamental skills |
| **Exploration-Exploitation Trade-off** | Adaptive reasoning | Recommender systems, multi-armed bandit problems | Balancing the use of known successful reasoning strategies (exploitation) with trying out new, uncertain strategies (exploration) |
| **Catastrophic Forgetting Mitigation** | Adaptive reasoning | Regular fine-tuning pipelines | Adjusting reasoning patterns via mechanisms like Elastic Weight Consolidation to preserve old capabilities when learning new ones |
