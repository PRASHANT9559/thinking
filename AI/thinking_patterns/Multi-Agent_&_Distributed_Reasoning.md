## Section 3: Multi-Agent & Distributed Reasoning

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Debate & Critique** | AI agents | Truth-seeking systems, fact verification | Multiple agents argue different positions or critique each other's outputs to reach consensus  |
| **Swarm Intelligence** | AI agents | Optimization problems, collective decision making | Decentralized agents follow simple local rules leading to emergent global intelligent behavior  |
| **Ensemble Reasoning** | AI agents | High-stakes predictions, medical diagnosis | Multiple agents with different specializations vote or combine outputs for robust decisions  |
| **Adversarial Multi-Agent** | AI agents | Security testing, robustness evaluation | Agents play opposing roles (attacker/defender) to stress-test solutions and find vulnerabilities |
| **Role-Playing Agents** | AI agents | Creative writing, scenario simulation | Agents adopt specific personas (researcher, critic, editor) to bring diverse perspectives |
| **Market-Based Reasoning** | AI agents | Resource allocation, task assignment | Agents bid or trade for tasks and resources, using economic mechanisms for coordination |
| **Consensus Building** | AI agents | Distributed systems, group decision making | Agents iteratively negotiate and converge on mutually acceptable solutions |
| **Specialist-Generalist Teams** | AI agents | Enterprise AI, research systems | Deep specialist agents handle specific domains while generalist agents coordinate and synthesize |
| **Recursive Agent Networks** | AI agents | Meta-learning systems, complex hierarchies | Agents can spawn sub-agents to handle subtasks, creating recursive delegation chains |
