## Section 8: Meta-Reasoning & Systems Thinking

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Systems Thinking** | Research concept | Complex problem analysis, sustainability AI | Analyzes interconnected components, feedback loops, and emergent properties of complex systems |
| **Second-Order Thinking** | Research concept | Consequence analysis, policy AI | Considers the consequences of consequences, thinking beyond immediate effects |
| **Probabilistic Thinking** | Research concept | Uncertainty quantification, Bayesian AI | Explicitly reasons with probabilities, updating beliefs based on evidence |
| **Inversion** | Research concept | Problem-solving, risk analysis | Solves problems by considering what to avoid rather than what to achieve |
| **First Principles Thinking** | Research concept | Physics-informed AI, innovation | Breaks problems down to fundamental truths and builds up solutions from there |
| **Lateral Thinking** | Research concept | Creative problem solving, innovation AI | Approaches problems from unexpected angles, challenging assumptions |
| **Reductionism vs Holism** | Research concept | Analysis vs synthesis tasks | Toggles between breaking problems into parts and considering the whole system |
| **Feedback Loop Analysis** | Research concept | Control systems, economic modeling | Identifies and analyzes reinforcing and balancing feedback loops in systems |
| **Emergence Recognition** | Research concept | Complex systems AI, game theory | Identifies when complex behaviors arise from simple component interactions |
| **Trade-off Analysis** | Research concept | Multi-objective optimization | Explicitly weighs competing objectives and opportunity costs in decisions |
| **Scenario Planning** | Research concept | Strategic AI, forecasting | Develops multiple plausible future scenarios and robust strategies for each |
| **Root Cause Analysis** | Research concept | Debugging, diagnostic systems | Uses techniques like 5 Whys or fishbone diagrams to identify fundamental causes |
