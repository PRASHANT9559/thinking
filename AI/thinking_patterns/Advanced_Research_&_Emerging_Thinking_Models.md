## Section 6: Advanced Research & Emerging Thinking Models

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Process-Based Supervision** | Research concept | OpenAI o1 training, math verification | Rewards intermediate reasoning steps, not just final outputs, to improve reasoning quality |
| **Test-Time Compute Scaling** | Research concept | OpenAI o3, DeepSeek-R1 | Allocates more computation time during inference for complex problems via extended thinking |
| **Reinforcement Learning for Reasoning** | Research concept | DeepSeek-R1, OpenAI o-series | Uses RL (not just SFT) to train models to discover effective reasoning strategies autonomously  |
| **Private Chain of Thought** | Research concept | OpenAI o3, secure reasoning systems | Internal reasoning that is not exposed in outputs, allowing more extensive deliberation  |
| **Speculative Reasoning** | Research concept | Fast inference systems, draft-then-verify | Generates quick speculative answers then verifies them with more expensive reasoning |
| **Mixture of Reasoners** | Research concept | Parallel reasoning architectures | Different "expert" reasoning modules handle different types of problems, selected dynamically |
| **Recursive Language Modeling** | Research concept | Meta-reasoning systems | Model can call itself recursively on subproblems, creating hierarchical reasoning processes |
| **Memory-Augmented Reasoning** | Research concept | Long-horizon tasks, continual learning | External memory stores (neural or symbolic) that reasoning can read from and write to |
| **Active Learning for Reasoning** | Research concept | Sample-efficient AI, curiosity-driven systems | Agent selects which problems to solve based on expected learning value |
| **Few-Shot In-Context Learning** | Research concept | Prompt engineering, adaptation | Learns new tasks from minimal examples provided in the context window |
| **Instruction Tuning** | Research concept | ChatGPT, Claude, instruction-following models | Fine-tuning on diverse instruction-response pairs to improve general task understanding |
| **Constitutional AI** | Research concept | Claude, alignment research | Uses AI feedback (AI critique and revision) based on principles to train helpful, harmless AI |
| **Debate-Based Training** | Research concept | AI safety, truth-seeking | Training regime where models debate to find truth, with human or AI judges |
| **Iterated Amplification** | Research concept | AI alignment, scalable oversight | Breaks complex problems into simpler subproblems that can be supervised more reliably |
| **Recursive Reward Modeling** | Research concept | Advanced alignment, meta-learning | Uses learned reward models that can themselves be improved through the same process |
