## Section 12: Ethical, Safety & Alignment Reasoning

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Constitutional Reasoning** | Ethical & Safety | Claude (Anthropic), aligned AI agents | Evaluating every potential output against a strict set of predefined principles (a "constitution") before generating a response |
| **Adversarial Red-Teaming** | Ethical & Safety | AI safety testing, jailbreak prevention | Reasoning specifically to identify vulnerabilities, edge cases, and safety bypasses in another model or system |
| **Value Alignment & Utility Weighting** | Ethical & Safety | Moral decision-making engines, policy systems | Weighing the outcomes of an action against human values (e.g., maximizing utility while minimizing harm) systematically |
| **Deontological Constraint Checking** | Ethical & Safety | Medical AI, strict compliance environments | Applying absolute rules ("Do no harm", "Never lie") that cannot be broken regardless of the potential positive outcome |
| **Harmlessness-Helpfulness Trade-off** | Ethical & Safety | RLHF-trained base models | Balancing the dual objectives of providing the most useful answer possible without providing dangerous or biased information |
| **Interpretability-Driven Reasoning** | Ethical & Safety | Transparent AI systems, audit logs | Enforcing that every step of reasoning must be output in human-readable and logically verifiable terms for auditing purposes |
| **Bias Mitigation & Fairness Auditing** | Ethical & Safety | Resume screening, loan approval algorithms | Actively reflecting on a generated conclusion to check if it disproportionately favors or harms specific protected demographic groups |
