## Section 4: Cognitive Science Inspired Reasoning

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Cognitive reasoning | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|---------------------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Dual Process Thinking** | Cognitive reasoning | System 1/Fast vs System 2/Slow AI architectures | Mimics Kahneman's dual process theory: fast intuitive responses vs slow deliberate reasoning |
| **Mental Simulation** | Cognitive reasoning | Robotics, physical reasoning | Internal simulation of outcomes before action, predicting consequences of potential actions |
| **Counterfactual Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Causal inference, decision support | Considers "what if" scenarios and alternative histories to improve decision making |
| **Abductive Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Diagnostic systems, scientific discovery | Infers the most likely explanation (hypothesis) from observed evidence |
| **Inductive Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Pattern recognition, generalization | Derives general principles from specific observations and examples |
| **Deductive Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Formal logic systems, theorem proving | Applies general rules to reach logically certain conclusions about specific cases |
| **Commonsense Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Social AI, human-robot interaction | Applies everyday world knowledge and physical intuition to situations |
| **Theory of Mind** | Cognitive reasoning | Multi-agent cooperation, social AI | Models the beliefs, intentions, and knowledge of other agents or humans |
| **Causal Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Intervention planning, root cause analysis | Understands cause-effect relationships beyond mere correlation |
| **Spatial Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Robotics, navigation, visual understanding | Represents and reasons about physical space, objects, and their relationships |
| **Temporal Reasoning** | Cognitive reasoning | Planning, forecasting, narrative understanding | Reasons about time, sequences, durations, and temporal constraints |
| **Metacognition** | Cognitive reasoning | Self-aware AI, learning to learn | Thinking about one's own thinking: monitoring, controlling, and evaluating cognitive processes |
| **Working Memory Management** | Cognitive reasoning | Long-context systems, conversation agents | Active maintenance and manipulation of information in short-term memory |
| **Cognitive Control** | Cognitive reasoning | Task switching, attention mechanisms | Executive functions that manage attention, inhibit irrelevant information, and switch tasks |
| **Intuitive Physics** | Cognitive reasoning | Object permanence, physical prediction | Innate understanding of physical laws (gravity, solidity, continuity) without explicit calculation |
