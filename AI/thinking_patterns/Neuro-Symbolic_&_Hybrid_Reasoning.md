## Section 5: Neuro-Symbolic & Hybrid Reasoning

| Thinking Pattern | Category | Where It Is Used | Short Explanation |
|------------------|----------|------------------|-------------------|
| **Neuro-Symbolic Integration** | Research concept | Amazon Bedrock, Nova 2 Lite, medical diagnosis | Combines neural pattern recognition with symbolic logical reasoning for verifiable, interpretable AI   |
| **Differentiable Logic** | Research concept | DeepProbLog, Logic Tensor Networks | Embeds logical operations in continuous spaces allowing gradient-based learning of symbolic rules  |
| **Neural Theorem Proving** | Research concept | Formal verification, mathematical AI | Uses neural networks to guide symbolic theorem provers through proof search spaces  |
| **Automated Reasoning Verification** | Research concept | Amazon Bedrock Guardrails, compliance systems | Uses formal logic and mathematical proof to mechanically verify AI outputs for correctness  |
| **Knowledge Graph Reasoning** | Research concept | Amazon Rufus, enterprise knowledge systems | Combines LLMs with structured knowledge graphs for grounded, explainable inference  |
| **Probabilistic Logic Programming** | Research concept | Uncertain reasoning, statistical relational AI | Extends logic programming with probability distributions for uncertain inference |
| **Neural Program Synthesis** | Research concept | AlphaCode, code generation systems | Generates executable programs from specifications using neural networks with symbolic constraints |
| **Scene Graph Reasoning** | Research concept | Visual question answering, robotics | Extracts symbolic scene representations from images for compositional reasoning |
| **Semantic Parsing** | Research concept | Natural language interfaces, database queries | Translates natural language to formal logical representations for precise execution |
| **Constraint Satisfaction** | Research concept | Scheduling, configuration, planning | Solves problems by finding solutions that satisfy a set of symbolic constraints |
| **Inductive Logic Programming** | Research concept | Scientific discovery, rule learning | Learns logical rules from examples and background knowledge |
| **Autoformalization** | Research concept | Amazon research, mathematical AI | Automatically converts natural language into formal logical or mathematical notation  |
| **Symbolic Regression** | Research concept | Scientific discovery, equation discovery | Discovers mathematical expressions that fit data while maintaining interpretability |
| **Neural-Symbolic Concept Learning** | Research concept | NS-CL (MIT), CLEVR benchmark | Jointly learns visual concepts and symbolic rules for compositional generalization  |
